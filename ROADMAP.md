# ENAF Roadmap

> **Transforming Business Strategy into Enterprise Network Excellence**

---

# Purpose

The ENAF Roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the Enterprise Network Architecture Framework.

Rather than representing a simple documentation plan, this roadmap describes how ENAF will mature into a comprehensive enterprise transformation framework that supports organisations throughout the entire lifecycle of network strategy, architecture, implementation, operations, governance, and continuous optimisation.

Each release builds upon the previous one while maintaining compatibility with the established methodology, repository structure, and governance principles.

---

# Vision

ENAF aims to become the reference framework for enterprise network transformation by integrating business strategy, enterprise architecture, programme delivery, operational excellence, and continuous improvement into a single practical methodology.

---

# Development Principles

The development of ENAF follows several guiding principles.

- Business value before technology
- Strategy before architecture
- Architecture before implementation
- Governance throughout the lifecycle
- Practical guidance over theoretical concepts
- Vendor-neutral methodology
- Framework integration rather than replacement
- Continuous improvement through real-world experience
- Stable repository structure
- Incremental and review-driven development

Every enhancement should strengthen the framework without compromising consistency or traceability.

# Long-Term Goals

The long-term objectives for ENAF include:

- Become a recognised enterprise transformation methodology.
- Bridge strategy and technical delivery.
- Support organisations across multiple industries.
- Integrate leading industry frameworks.
- Enable measurable business outcomes.
- Build a practical knowledge base for enterprise architects.
- Support education, certification, and community contributions.
