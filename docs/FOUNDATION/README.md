# ENAF Foundation

> **Establishing the principles, philosophy, and structure of the Enterprise Network Architecture Framework**

---

# Purpose

The Foundation introduces the concepts that underpin the Enterprise Network Architecture Framework (ENAF).

Before organisations can successfully apply the ENAF lifecycle, they must first understand the philosophy, principles, governance model, and architectural approach that guide every decision throughout enterprise transformation.

The Foundation establishes this common understanding.

It explains **what ENAF is**, **why it exists**, **how it should be applied**, and **how it integrates with recognised industry frameworks**.

Together, these documents provide the knowledge required before beginning the detailed lifecycle stages.

---

# Foundation Objectives

The Foundation aims to:

- Explain the purpose of ENAF.
- Define the scope of the framework.
- Establish the guiding principles.
- Introduce the enterprise transformation lifecycle.
- Describe the governance model.
- Explain how ENAF integrates with recognised frameworks.
- Define the standard deliverables produced throughout the lifecycle.
- Provide guidance for applying ENAF in practice.

# Relationship to the Lifecycle

The Foundation does not contain detailed implementation guidance.

Instead, it introduces the concepts that are applied throughout every lifecycle stage.

For example:

- The Guiding Principles influence every architectural decision.
- The Governance Model applies across the entire lifecycle.
- The Architecture Lifecycle provides the transformation structure.
- Framework Mapping explains how ENAF works alongside recognised industry standards.
- Architecture Deliverables define the expected outputs from each lifecycle stage.

The detailed activities, inputs, outputs, compliance requirements, governance checkpoints, and case studies are documented within the **LIFECYCLE** section.

# Key Concepts

Throughout the Foundation, several themes remain consistent:

- Business strategy drives enterprise transformation.
- Architecture enables business capabilities.
- Governance spans the entire lifecycle.
- Transformation is delivered through programmes and projects.
- Operations sustain delivered capabilities.
- Continuous optimisation improves long-term business value.
- Every activity maintains traceability to an approved business objective.

These principles underpin every part of ENAF.
