# Architecture Deliverables

## Purpose

The Enterprise Network Architecture Framework (ENAF) defines a standard set of deliverables that support every stage of the enterprise transformation lifecycle.

These deliverables provide the information, decisions, governance, and documentation required to transform business strategy into operational enterprise capabilities.

Rather than producing documentation for its own sake, every ENAF deliverable exists to support decision-making, governance, delivery, operations, or continuous improvement.

Each deliverable should have a clear business purpose, an identified owner, and measurable value.

---

# Why Deliverables Matter

Enterprise transformation depends on consistent and trusted information.

Without defined deliverables, organisations often experience:

- Inconsistent documentation
- Duplicate information
- Missing architectural decisions
- Poor governance
- Difficult project handovers
- Limited traceability
- Knowledge loss
- Operational risk

Standard deliverables establish a common language across architects, programme teams, project managers, operational teams, and executive stakeholders.

They also ensure that transformation activities remain repeatable, governed, and measurable.

---

# ENAF Deliverable Principles

Every deliverable should be:

- Business focused
- Fit for purpose
- Accurate and current
- Version controlled
- Traceable
- Governed
- Reusable
- Easy to understand
- Owned by a responsible stakeholder
- Reviewed throughout the lifecycle

Documentation should support transformation—not become an administrative burden.

# Deliverables Across the Lifecycle

Every ENAF lifecycle stage produces a defined set of outputs.

## Strategy

Purpose

Establish strategic direction and business priorities.

Typical deliverables:

- Enterprise Vision
- Business Drivers
- Strategic Objectives
- Success Measures
- Business Capabilities
- Transformation Initiatives
- Stakeholder Analysis

---

## Current State

Purpose

Establish the enterprise baseline.

Typical deliverables:

- Current State Assessment
- Business Architecture Assessment
- Application Assessment
- Infrastructure Assessment
- Network Assessment
- Security Assessment
- Technology Inventory
- Risk Assessment
- Gap Analysis

---

## Target State

Purpose

Define the future operating model.

Typical deliverables:

- Target Business Capabilities
- Target Operating Model
- Target Architecture
- Target Network Architecture
- Technology Standards
- Design Principles
- Success Criteria

---

## Architecture

Purpose

Develop the enterprise architecture required to achieve the target state.

Typical deliverables:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Domain Architectures
- Reference Architectures
- Solution Patterns
- Architecture Standards
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
- Design Specifications

---

## Roadmap

Purpose

Plan enterprise transformation.

Typical deliverables:

- Transformation Roadmap
- Transition Architectures
- Programme Roadmap
- Project Portfolio
- Transformation Waves
- Dependency Maps
- Investment Priorities
- Migration Strategy

---

## Implementation

Purpose

Deliver approved transformation initiatives.

Typical deliverables:

- Programme Charter
- Project Charter
- Delivery Plans
- Release Plans
- Sprint Plans
- Architecture Compliance Assessments
- Operational Readiness Assessment
- Benefits Realisation Plan

---

## Operations

Purpose

Operate and support delivered capabilities.

Typical deliverables:

- Service Catalogue
- Operational Procedures
- Monitoring Dashboards
- Incident Reports
- Performance Reports
- Capacity Plans
- Service Reviews
- Operational Metrics

---

## Governance

Purpose

Provide oversight and assurance.

Typical deliverables:

- Governance Policies

- Compliance Assessments
- Risk Registers

- Audit Reports

- Performance Improvements
- Benefits Reviews

# Deliverable Ownership

Every deliverable should have a clearly defined owner.

| Deliverable Type | Typical Owner |
|------------------|---------------|
| Strategy | Executive Leadership |
| Enterprise Architecture | Enterprise Architect |
| Domain Architecture | Domain Architect |
| Programme Deliverables | Programme Manager |
| Project Deliverables | Project Manager |
| Operational Deliverables | Operations Manager |
| Governance Deliverables | Governance Board |
| Compliance Deliverables | Risk and Compliance Teams |
| Optimisation Deliverables | Enterprise Architecture Team |

Ownership ensures accountability throughout the lifecycle.

---

# Measuring Deliverable Quality

High-quality deliverables should demonstrate:

- Business relevance
- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Traceability
- Governance compliance
- Operational usefulness
- Ease of maintenance

Documentation should enable better decisions rather than simply record information.

---

# Summary

ENAF deliverables provide the knowledge, decisions, and governance required to support enterprise transformation throughout the entire lifecycle.

By defining consistent deliverables for every stage, ENAF enables organisations to maintain traceability from business strategy through architecture, programme delivery, operations, governance, and continuous optimisation.

Each deliverable contributes to a common objective: transforming business strategy into measurable business value through structured enterprise network architecture.
