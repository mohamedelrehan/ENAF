# Activities

The Architecture stage consists of a structured set of activities that transform the approved Target State into a governed enterprise architecture.

These activities define the architectural principles, standards, reference architectures, technology standards, design patterns, and governance models that will guide all future solution designs and transformation initiatives.

Each activity contributes to one or more Architecture deliverables that collectively provide the enterprise architectural blueprint used throughout the remainder of the ENAF lifecycle.

Although organisations may tailor these activities to suit their governance model, the overall sequence should remain consistent to ensure a repeatable, governed, and traceable approach to enterprise architecture.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Architecture activities is to develop a complete enterprise architecture that enables consistent solution design while ensuring alignment with business strategy, the approved Target State, and enterprise governance.

By completing these activities, Enterprise Architects produce the Architecture deliverables required for governance review and approval before progressing to the Roadmap stage.

---

# Activity 1 – Develop the Business Architecture

## Objective

Develop the detailed business architecture that supports the approved future business model and provides guidance for future solution design.

---

### Typical Activities

- Refine business capabilities.
- Develop business service models.
- Define business functions.
- Model business interactions.
- Identify business dependencies.
- Validate business architecture.

---

### Questions to Ask

- Does the architecture support future business capabilities?
- Are business services clearly defined?
- Have organisational dependencies been identified?
- Is the business architecture complete?

---

### Participants

- Enterprise Architect
- Business Architects
- Business Owners
- Executive Sponsor

---

### Deliverables Produced

| Deliverable | Contribution |
|-------------|--------------|
| DEL-AR-01 Business Architecture | Defines the enterprise business architecture. |
| DEL-AR-09 Architecture Summary | Summarises the business architecture. |

# Activity 7 – Validate the Enterprise Architecture

## Objective

Validate that the enterprise architecture is complete, internally consistent, aligned with the approved Target State, and ready for governance approval.

---

### Typical Activities

- Review all Architecture deliverables.
- Validate architectural consistency.
- Confirm alignment with Target State.
- Confirm compliance with enterprise standards.
- Obtain stakeholder agreement.
- Prepare deliverables for governance review.

---

### Validation Questions

- Does the enterprise architecture fully support the Target State?
- Are enterprise standards complete?
- Are reference architectures consistent?
- Is stakeholder agreement confirmed?
- Are all Architecture deliverables complete?

If the answer to any of these questions is **No**, additional architecture work should be completed before requesting governance approval.

# Summary

The Architecture stage is complete when all Architecture activities have been performed and the required Architecture deliverables have been produced.

These deliverables provide the architectural evidence required for the Architecture Decision Gate (**AR-DG-01**), where they are formally reviewed before progressing to the Roadmap stage.
