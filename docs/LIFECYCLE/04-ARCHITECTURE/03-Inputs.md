# Inputs

The Architecture stage begins after the successful completion and approval of the Target State stage.

Its purpose is to transform the approved future-state architecture into a detailed enterprise architecture consisting of reference architectures, architecture principles, standards, design patterns, and technology guidance that can be consistently applied across the organisation.

Only approved outputs from previous lifecycle stages should be used as inputs to ensure governance, traceability, and architectural consistency throughout the ENAF lifecycle.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify the mandatory inputs required before beginning the Architecture stage.

These inputs provide the approved business direction, validated current environment, authorised future-state architecture, and governance decisions required to produce the enterprise architecture.

---

# Input Validation

Before beginning the Architecture stage, confirm that:

- All Strategy outputs have been approved through **STR-DG-01**.
- All Current State outputs have been approved through **CS-DG-01**.
- All Target State outputs have been approved through **TS-DG-01**.
- The approved outputs are complete and available.
- Business objectives remain valid.
- The approved Target State accurately reflects the future business direction.
- Architectural assumptions and constraints remain valid.
- Executive sponsorship remains in place.

If any of these conditions are not met, the relevant lifecycle stage should be reviewed before proceeding.

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Architect is responsible for ensuring that only approved lifecycle outputs are used as Architecture inputs.

No draft documents, working papers, or unapproved architectural designs should be used when developing the enterprise architecture.

This governance approach ensures that every architecture principle, standard, reference architecture, and design decision can be traced back to an approved business objective, validated assessment, or authorised Target State deliverable.
