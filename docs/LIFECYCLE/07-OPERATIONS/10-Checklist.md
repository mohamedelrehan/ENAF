# Operations Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist validates that the Operations stage has been completed successfully and that the organisation is capable of sustaining business services, protecting enterprise architecture, and continuously improving operational performance.

Unlike the Implementation Checklist, which confirms successful project delivery, the Operations Checklist confirms that the organisation is fully prepared to operate the transformed enterprise as part of normal business activities.

Completion of this checklist provides evidence that enterprise operations are stable, repeatable, measurable, and aligned with business objectives.

---

# How to Use This Checklist

This checklist should be completed:

- Before the Operations Decision Gate.
- During Operational Readiness Reviews.
- Following major transformation programmes.
- During internal operational assessments.
- Before governance reviews.
- As part of periodic operational maturity assessments.

Every item should be reviewed and supported by appropriate evidence.

Recommended status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| ✅ Complete | Requirement fully satisfied |
| ⚠️ Partial | Requirement partially implemented or improvement required |
| ❌ Not Complete | Requirement not yet implemented |
| N/A | Not applicable |

# Completion Criteria

The Operations stage is considered complete when:

- Operational ownership has been formally established.
- Business services are operating within agreed service levels.
- Governance and compliance processes are active.
- Monitoring and operational reporting are fully operational.
- Continuous Improvement mechanisms have been established.
- The organisation demonstrates the capability to operate, govern, and improve the enterprise independently of the transformation programme.

Successful completion of this checklist provides the evidence required to pass the Operations Decision Gate and transition into the Governance stage of the Enterprise Network Architecture Framework (ENAF).
