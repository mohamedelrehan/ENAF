# Decision Gate

## Purpose

The Operations Decision Gate confirms that the organisation has successfully transitioned from programme delivery into stable enterprise operations.

Unlike previous lifecycle stages, Operations is a continuous capability that does not have a fixed completion date.

This decision gate does not indicate the end of Operations.

Instead, it confirms that operational capabilities have reached an agreed level of maturity, stability, governance, and performance that enables the Governance stage to assume ongoing enterprise oversight.

The objective is to ensure that business services are operating reliably, operational processes are established, compliance is maintained, and continuous improvement has become part of normal business operations.

---

# Decision Statement

The Operations stage may be considered operationally established when:

- Enterprise services are stable.
- Operational responsibilities are clearly defined.
- Business service levels are consistently achieved.
- Governance processes are operating effectively.
- Compliance activities are embedded into daily operations.
- Operational performance is measurable.
- Continuous improvement processes are active.

Once these conditions are satisfied, Governance becomes the primary oversight capability while Operations continues to deliver enterprise services.

---

# Decision Criteria

The following criteria should be reviewed before approving the Operations Decision Gate.

---

## Business Operations

Verify that:

- Business-critical services are operational.
- Service ownership has been assigned.
- Business stakeholders formally accept operational ownership.
- Operational support aligns with business priorities.
- Business continuity arrangements are operational.

---

## Service Operations

Confirm that:

- Operational procedures are documented.
- Service Catalogue is maintained.
- Service Level Agreements are monitored.
- Incident Management is operational.
- Problem Management is operational.
- Change Enablement is operational.
- Operational support model is established.

---

## Infrastructure and Platform Operations

Verify that:

- Infrastructure is fully supported.
- Configuration baselines are maintained.
- Backup and recovery procedures are operational.
- Capacity is continuously monitored.
- Platform lifecycle management is established.

---

## Network Operations

Confirm that:

- Enterprise connectivity is fully operational.
- Core network services are monitored.
- Cloud connectivity is stable.
- Remote access services operate reliably.
- Network documentation is maintained.
- Network resilience has been validated.

---

## Security Operations

Verify that:

- Security monitoring is active.
- Identity and Access Management is operational.
- Vulnerability management is established.
- Security incident response is operational.
- Security baselines are maintained.
- Operational security reviews are performed.

---

## Monitoring and Observability

Confirm that:

- Monitoring coverage is complete.
- Dashboards are available.
- Alerting has been validated.
- Operational reporting is active.
- Root cause analysis processes are established.

---

## Automation

Verify that:

- Operational automation is functioning.
- Infrastructure as Code repositories are maintained.
- Automated operational workflows are documented.
- Manual operational effort is continuously reduced.

---

## Knowledge Management

Confirm that:

- Runbooks are complete.
- Knowledge articles are maintained.
- Operational procedures are documented.
- Lessons learned are captured.
- Knowledge transfer has been completed.

---

## Governance Readiness

Verify that:

- Governance reporting is operational.
- Operational KPIs are measured.
- Architecture compliance is monitored.
- Risks are actively managed.
- Operational decisions are traceable.
- Exception management is established.

---

## Compliance Readiness

Confirm that:

- Compliance monitoring is active.
- Required audit evidence exists.
- Internal policies are followed.
- Regulatory obligations are maintained.
- Compliance reporting is operational.

---

## Performance Management

Verify that:

- Operational KPIs are defined.
- Service performance is measurable.
- Capacity trends are monitored.
- Customer satisfaction is reviewed.
- Operational maturity is measured.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Confirm that:

- Improvement opportunities are recorded.
- Technical debt is managed.
- Automation opportunities are identified.
- Operational reviews occur regularly.
- Improvement plans are actively maintained.

---

# Decision Outcomes

Following the assessment, one of the following outcomes should be recorded.

## Approved

Operations has achieved the required level of maturity.

Governance assumes ongoing enterprise oversight while Operations continues delivering business services.

---

## Approved with Recommendations

Operations is stable but improvement opportunities have been identified.

These recommendations should be incorporated into the Continuous Improvement Register and monitored through Governance.

---

## Conditional Approval

Operations is functional but significant issues remain.

Formal corrective actions must be completed within agreed timescales.

Governance should monitor progress until all conditions have been satisfied.

---

## Not Approved

Operations has not yet achieved the required operational maturity.

Critical operational issues must be resolved before the organisation can transition to full operational governance.

---

# Evidence Required

Decision makers should review evidence including:

- Service performance reports.
- Operational dashboards.
- KPI reports.
- Incident trends.
- Problem trends.
- Security reports.
- Compliance reports.
- Audit evidence.
- Risk Register.
- Architecture Compliance Reports.
- Service Review Reports.
- Knowledge Base.
- Runbooks.
- Continuous Improvement Register.
- Operational metrics.

Evidence should demonstrate sustainable operational capability rather than temporary project success.

---

# Decision Authority

Approval should normally be granted by representatives from:

- Business Leadership.
- Operations Management.
- Enterprise Architecture.
- Information Security.
- Governance.
- Service Management.
- Programme Leadership (where applicable).

The approving authority may vary according to organisational governance structures.

---

# Outputs

Approval of the Operations Decision Gate provides:

- Confirmation of stable operational capability.
- Operational maturity baseline.
- Governance readiness.
- Compliance assurance.
- Operational performance baseline.
- Continuous Improvement baseline.
- Formal transition into ongoing enterprise governance.

These outputs become primary inputs to the Governance stage of the ENAF lifecycle.

---

# Summary

The Operations Decision Gate confirms that the organisation has successfully established a mature, stable, secure, and governed operational capability.

Unlike earlier lifecycle decision gates, this gate does not represent the completion of a project.

Instead, it confirms that Operations has become a sustainable enterprise capability capable of delivering long-term business value while supporting governance, compliance, performance management, and continuous improvement.

Successful completion of this decision gate enables Governance to focus on strategic oversight, risk management, policy enforcement, and enterprise decision-making while Operations continues to deliver secure, resilient, and business-aligned services.
