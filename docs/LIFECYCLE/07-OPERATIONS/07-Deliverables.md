# Deliverables

## Purpose

The Operations stage produces and maintains the operational capabilities required to support enterprise services throughout their lifecycle.

Unlike implementation deliverables, which are typically project outputs, operational deliverables are living assets that evolve continuously as the organisation grows, changes, and improves.

These deliverables provide the operational foundation for governance, compliance, optimisation, and future transformation initiatives.

---

# Operational Deliverable Categories

Operational deliverables are grouped into the following categories:

- Business Deliverables
- Service Deliverables
- Infrastructure Deliverables
- Network Deliverables
- Security Deliverables
- Monitoring Deliverables
- Automation Deliverables
- Knowledge Deliverables
- Governance Deliverables
- Compliance Deliverables
- Performance Deliverables
- Improvement Deliverables

Together these deliverables represent the operational capability of the enterprise rather than individual project outputs.

---

# Business Deliverables

Operations maintains several business-facing deliverables that ensure enterprise services continue to support organisational objectives.

Typical business deliverables include:

- Service Catalogue
- Business Service Portfolio
- Service Ownership Register
- Business Continuity Documentation
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Operational Support Model
- Customer Communication Plans

These deliverables ensure that business services remain clearly defined, supported, and aligned with business expectations.

- Governance evidence.
- Compliance evidence.
- Service metrics.
- Security metrics.
- Risk information.

- Business feedback.

---

# Success Criteria

The Operations stage is considered successful when:

- Enterprise services operate reliably and securely.
- Business service levels are consistently achieved.
- Operational processes are standardised and repeatable.
- Architecture integrity is maintained.
- Compliance obligations are continuously met.
- Operational performance is measurable.
- Knowledge is effectively managed.
- Automation reduces operational effort.
- Improvement opportunities are continuously identified.
- Operational deliverables support governance and future transformation.

---

# Summary

The deliverables produced during the Operations stage represent the enterprise's operational capability rather than the outputs of a temporary project.

They provide the documentation, operational intelligence, governance evidence, technical assets, automation capabilities, and business information required to sustain enterprise services over the long term.

Within ENAF, these deliverables ensure that Operations remains a strategic business capability, providing the information and maturity needed to support Governance, Optimisation, and future transformation initiatives.
