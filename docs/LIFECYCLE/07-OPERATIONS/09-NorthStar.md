# NorthStar Case Study

## Purpose

This case study demonstrates how the Operations stage of the Enterprise Network Architecture Framework (ENAF) is applied within a global enterprise following the successful completion of an enterprise transformation programme.

Having completed the Strategy, Current State, Target State, Architecture, Roadmap, and Implementation stages, NorthStar Retail Group transitions into Business-as-Usual (BAU) operations.

The objective is no longer to deliver technology, but to operate, govern, and continuously improve the enterprise while ensuring that technology continues to support business objectives.

---

## Operational Transition

Following programme closure, responsibility for the new enterprise environment is formally transferred from the Transformation Programme to the Operations organisation.

This transition includes:

- Establishing operational ownership.
- Completing knowledge transfer.
- Validating operational readiness.
- Activating operational governance.
- Confirming service support arrangements.
- Enabling continuous monitoring and reporting.

The Executive Board approves the transition after confirming that people, processes, technology, governance, and support capabilities are in place.

---

## Building Operational Capability

NorthStar establishes a global operating model consisting of:

- Service Management
- Network Operations
- Cloud Operations
- Security Operations
- Platform Engineering
- Enterprise Architecture
- Business Relationship Management

Each function has clearly defined responsibilities while working within a common governance and operational framework.

---

## Operating the Enterprise

Operations focuses on maintaining reliable, secure, and resilient business services.

Daily activities include:

- Monitoring enterprise services.
- Managing incidents and changes.
- Protecting the enterprise through security operations.
- Maintaining architecture standards.
- Measuring operational performance.
- Supporting business users.
- Automating repetitive activities.
- Managing operational risks.

Rather than reacting to technology failures, Operations works proactively to prevent disruption and improve service quality.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Operational data is continuously analysed to identify opportunities for improvement.

Lessons learned from incidents, performance trends, customer feedback, and operational metrics are captured within the Enterprise Improvement Register.

These improvements influence future architecture decisions, governance activities, and investment planning.

---

## Outcome

NorthStar successfully establishes a mature operational capability that:

- Sustains the value delivered during implementation.
- Protects enterprise architecture.
- Supports business growth.
- Improves operational efficiency.
- Reduces enterprise risk.
- Provides measurable business value.
- Generates strategic insights for future transformation.

The Operations stage demonstrates that enterprise transformation does not end with implementation.

Instead, it becomes part of a continuous lifecycle where operational experience informs governance, optimisation, and the next strategic planning cycle.
