# Activities

## Purpose

The Governance stage establishes the activities required to provide continuous oversight, strategic direction, accountability, and control across the enterprise.

Unlike the Implementation stage, which delivers change, or the Operations stage, which runs day-to-day services, Governance focuses on ensuring that the organisation continues to operate in alignment with its business strategy, enterprise architecture, policies, and regulatory obligations.

These activities provide the mechanisms through which executives, enterprise architects, business leaders, and operational teams evaluate performance, make informed decisions, manage risk, and guide the future evolution of the enterprise.

---

# Governance Activity Overview

The Governance stage consists of the following core activities:

1. Establish Governance Structures
2. Monitor Enterprise Performance
3. Review Architecture Compliance
4. Manage Enterprise Risk
5. Govern Change and Investment
6. Ensure Compliance
7. Review Business Value
8. Drive Continuous Improvement
9. Report to Executive Leadership
10. Prepare for Optimisation

These activities operate continuously throughout the enterprise lifecycle.

---

# 1. Establish Governance Structures

Create the organisational bodies responsible for enterprise oversight and decision-making.

Typical activities include:

- Define governance operating model.
- Establish governance boards.
- Assign decision authority.
- Define roles and responsibilities.
- Create governance policies.
- Establish review schedules.
- Define approval processes.
- Publish governance framework.

**Key Deliverables**

- Governance Model
- Governance Charter
- RACI Matrix
- Governance Calendar

---

# 2. Monitor Enterprise Performance

Continuously measure whether enterprise services, technology, and business capabilities are delivering expected outcomes.

Typical activities include:

- Review business KPIs.
- Monitor operational dashboards.
- Analyse service performance.
- Review customer satisfaction.
- Track programme benefits.
- Measure business outcomes.
- Identify performance trends.
- Escalate significant issues.

**Key Deliverables**

- Executive Dashboard
- Performance Reports
- KPI Scorecards

---

# 3. Review Architecture Compliance

Ensure enterprise solutions continue to align with approved architecture principles, standards, and reference architectures.

Typical activities include:

- Conduct architecture reviews.
- Assess solution compliance.
- Review Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).
- Evaluate technology lifecycle.
- Manage architecture exceptions.
- Identify architectural drift.
- Recommend corrective actions.

**Key Deliverables**

- Architecture Compliance Reports
- Exception Register
- Architecture Review Findings

---

# 4. Manage Enterprise Risk

Identify, assess, prioritise, and monitor enterprise risks that may affect strategic objectives or business operations.

Typical activities include:

- Maintain Enterprise Risk Register.
- Review operational risks.
- Assess cyber risks.
- Evaluate vendor risks.
- Monitor business continuity readiness.
- Track mitigation activities.
- Escalate high-impact risks.

**Key Deliverables**

- Enterprise Risk Register
- Risk Assessments
- Risk Treatment Plans

---

# 5. Govern Change and Investment

Ensure that enterprise changes and investments remain aligned with strategic priorities and architectural direction.

Typical activities include:

- Review major change proposals.
- Evaluate investment requests.
- Prioritise initiatives.
- Assess business cases.
- Approve strategic programmes.
- Review technology lifecycle decisions.
- Monitor portfolio performance.

**Key Deliverables**

- Investment Decisions
- Portfolio Reviews
- Change Approval Records

---

# 6. Ensure Compliance

Confirm that enterprise operations continue to comply with internal policies, industry standards, contractual obligations, and regulatory requirements.

Typical activities include:

- Review compliance status.
- Monitor audit findings.
- Track corrective actions.
- Assess policy adherence.
- Review certification status.
- Validate regulatory reporting.
- Govern compliance exceptions.

**Key Deliverables**

- Compliance Reports
- Audit Findings
- Corrective Action Plans

---

# 7. Review Business Value

Measure whether transformation investments continue to deliver measurable business benefits.

Typical activities include:

- Review benefit realisation.
- Compare expected versus actual outcomes.
- Evaluate operational efficiency.
- Review financial performance.
- Assess customer experience.
- Identify value improvement opportunities.

**Key Deliverables**

- Benefit Realisation Reports
- Business Value Assessments
- Executive Reviews

---

# 8. Drive Continuous Improvement

Use governance findings to improve enterprise performance, maturity, and strategic capability.

Typical activities include:

- Review lessons learned.
- Prioritise improvement initiatives.
- Evaluate innovation opportunities.
- Track improvement programmes.
- Measure improvement outcomes.
- Update governance practices.

**Key Deliverables**

- Continuous Improvement Register
- Improvement Roadmap
- Lessons Learned Register

---

# 9. Report to Executive Leadership

Provide leadership with clear, evidence-based information to support strategic decision-making.

Typical activities include:

- Prepare executive reports.
- Present governance findings.
- Summarise enterprise performance.
- Highlight strategic risks.
- Recommend investment priorities.
- Escalate critical issues.
- Support executive reviews.

**Key Deliverables**

- Executive Governance Reports
- Board Presentations
- Strategic Recommendations

---

# 10. Prepare for Optimisation

Identify opportunities that will improve enterprise capabilities and feed directly into the Optimisation stage.

Typical activities include:

- Review enterprise maturity.
- Identify capability gaps.
- Evaluate emerging technologies.
- Recommend optimisation initiatives.
- Update strategic priorities.
- Prepare future investment recommendations.

**Key Deliverables**

- Optimisation Opportunities
- Capability Improvement Plan
- Strategic Recommendations

---

# Governance Operating Cycle

Governance is a continuous management process rather than a one-time review.

```text
Measure Performance
        │
        ▼
Review Architecture
        │
        ▼
Assess Risk & Compliance
        │
        ▼
Review Business Value
        │
        ▼
Make Governance Decisions
        │
        ▼
Approve Improvements
        │
        ▼
Monitor Outcomes
        │
        └───────────────┐
                        ▼
                Continuous Governance
```

# Governance Participants

Governance activities typically involve representatives from across the enterprise.

| Role | Primary Responsibility |
|------|-------------------------|
| Executive Leadership | Strategic direction and investment decisions |
| Enterprise Architecture | Architecture governance and standards |
| Business Leadership | Business priorities and value realisation |
| Operations | Operational performance and service delivery |
| Security | Cyber risk and security governance |
| Risk & Compliance | Enterprise risk and regulatory compliance |
| Finance | Investment governance and financial oversight |
| Programme Management | Portfolio governance and transformation reporting |

---

# Success Indicators

The Governance activities are effective when:

- Governance boards operate consistently.
- Enterprise performance is regularly reviewed.
- Architecture compliance remains high.
- Risks are actively managed.
- Regulatory obligations are consistently met.
- Investment decisions are evidence-based.
- Business value is measurable.
- Improvement initiatives are prioritised and delivered.
- Executive leadership has clear visibility of enterprise performance.

---

# Relationship to the ENAF Lifecycle

Governance activities use outputs from every previous lifecycle stage to evaluate enterprise performance and guide future decisions.

The findings from Governance become key inputs to the **Optimisation** stage, where improvement opportunities are prioritised, enterprise capabilities are enhanced, and the organisation prepares for the next cycle of strategic transformation.

---

# Summary

The Governance stage transforms information into action through structured oversight, informed decision-making, and continuous accountability.

By establishing repeatable governance activities, organisations ensure that business strategy, enterprise architecture, operational performance, risk management, and investment decisions remain aligned throughout the enterprise lifecycle.

Within ENAF, these activities provide the discipline required to sustain transformation, protect enterprise value, and enable continuous evolution.
