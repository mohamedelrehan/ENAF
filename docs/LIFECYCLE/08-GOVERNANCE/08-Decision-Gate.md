# Decision Gate

## Purpose

The Governance Decision Gate is the formal review point that confirms whether the organisation has established an effective governance capability capable of sustaining enterprise transformation over the long term.

Unlike previous decision gates, which approve progression to the next delivery phase, the Governance Decision Gate evaluates whether the enterprise has the leadership, oversight, accountability, and control mechanisms required to continuously govern business operations, technology investments, enterprise architecture, and organisational change.

Successful completion of this gate demonstrates that governance has become an embedded organisational capability rather than a project activity.

---

# Governance Decision

The Governance Board determines whether:

- Business strategy continues to guide enterprise decisions.
- Governance structures are fully operational.
- Enterprise Architecture is actively governed.
- Operational performance is being monitored.
- Enterprise risks are effectively managed.
- Regulatory and policy compliance is maintained.
- Technology investments are governed.
- Executive reporting supports informed decision-making.
- Continuous improvement is embedded within governance processes.

Only when these conditions are satisfied should the organisation proceed to the Optimisation stage.

---

# Governance Review Areas

The Governance Board should evaluate the following areas.

| Area | Review Focus |
|------|--------------|
| Strategic Alignment | Are business and technology decisions aligned with enterprise strategy? |
| Governance Model | Are governance structures operating effectively? |
| Enterprise Architecture | Are architecture principles and standards being maintained? |
| Operational Performance | Are business services achieving agreed performance objectives? |
| Risk Management | Are enterprise risks actively monitored and managed? |
| Compliance | Are regulatory and policy obligations being met? |
| Investment Governance | Are technology investments delivering measurable business value? |
| Executive Reporting | Do executives have sufficient visibility to support decision-making? |
| Continuous Improvement | Are governance outcomes driving measurable improvements? |

---

# Decision Criteria

The Governance stage is considered complete when the following criteria have been achieved.

## Strategic Governance

- Enterprise strategy remains current.
- Governance aligns business and technology decisions.
- Executive sponsorship is active.
- Strategic objectives are measurable.

---

## Governance Structure

- Governance forums are established.
- Decision authorities are clearly defined.
- Roles and responsibilities are understood.
- Governance meetings occur regularly.

---

## Enterprise Architecture

- Architecture principles are actively enforced.
- Architecture compliance is monitored.
- Exceptions follow an approval process.
- Technology standards remain current.

---

## Operational Governance

- Operational performance is measured.
- Service KPIs are reviewed.
- Operational issues are escalated appropriately.
- Governance supports operational decision-making.

---

## Risk and Compliance

- Enterprise Risk Register is maintained.
- High-priority risks have mitigation plans.
- Compliance is continuously monitored.
- Audit findings are addressed.

---

## Financial Governance

- Investment decisions align with business priorities.
- Benefits are measured.
- Portfolio performance is reviewed.
- Financial governance supports optimisation.

---

## Continuous Improvement

- Improvement opportunities are captured.
- Lessons learned are reviewed.
- Governance recommendations are tracked.
- Future optimisation initiatives are identified.

# Decision Outcomes

## Approved

Governance capability is operating effectively.

The organisation proceeds to the **Optimisation** stage.

---

## Approved with Actions

Governance capability is operational, but minor improvements remain.

Improvement actions are assigned and monitored while Optimisation activities begin.

---

## Deferred

Governance capability requires additional maturity before progressing.

The Governance Board defines corrective actions and schedules a follow-up review.

---

## Rejected

Critical governance gaps prevent progression.

Major deficiencies must be addressed before the Governance stage can be considered complete.

---

# Governance Board Participants

The Governance Decision Gate should normally include representatives from:

- Executive Leadership
- Business Leadership
- Enterprise Architecture
- Operations
- Security
- Risk and Compliance
- Finance
- Portfolio / PMO
- Service Management

Additional stakeholders may participate depending on the organisation's structure and regulatory requirements.

---

# Outputs

Successful completion of the Governance Decision Gate produces:

- Governance Approval
- Executive Governance Report
- Governance Decision Record
- Updated Enterprise Risk Register
- Architecture Compliance Summary
- Continuous Improvement Register
- Optimisation Recommendation List

These outputs become the primary inputs to the Optimisation stage.

---

# Relationship to the ENAF Lifecycle

The Governance Decision Gate represents the transition from **enterprise oversight** to **enterprise enhancement**.

At this point, the organisation has demonstrated that:

- Strategy continues to guide decision-making.
- Enterprise Architecture is protected.
- Operations are effectively governed.
- Risks and compliance are actively managed.
- Investment decisions are evidence-based.
- Governance is driving continual improvement.

The focus now shifts from governing the enterprise to optimising its capabilities, performance, and long-term value.

---

# Summary

The Governance Decision Gate confirms that governance has become an established organisational capability rather than a temporary programme activity.

By validating strategic alignment, executive oversight, architecture governance, operational performance, risk management, compliance, and continuous improvement, this gate provides confidence that the enterprise is ready to enter the Optimisation stage and continue its transformation through ongoing enhancement and innovation.
