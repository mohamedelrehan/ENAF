# Deliverables

The Target State stage produces the approved architectural deliverables that define the organisation's desired future-state enterprise architecture.

These deliverables describe how the organisation intends to operate after the transformation, providing a clear architectural vision across business, applications, data, technology, security, and network domains.

Together, they establish the architectural baseline that will be reviewed during the Target State Decision Gate (**TS-DG-01**) before progressing to the Architecture stage.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Target State deliverables is to document the future-state enterprise architecture in a consistent, governed, and traceable manner.

These deliverables provide:

- A shared vision of the future enterprise.
- Alignment between business strategy and technology.
- Architectural guidance for solution design.
- Inputs for transformation planning.
- Evidence for governance approval.

---

# Deliverables

## DEL-TS-01 – Future Business Architecture

### Purpose

Defines how the organisation will operate in the future to achieve its strategic objectives.

### Typical Contents

- Future Business Capability Model

- Business Services
- Business Outcomes

# Deliverable Validation

Before requesting governance approval, verify that:

- All Target State activities have been completed.
- Every required DEL-TS deliverable has been produced.
- Deliverables are internally consistent.
- Business objectives are fully supported.
- Current State gaps have been addressed.
- Stakeholders have reviewed the future architecture.
- Architectural assumptions and constraints have been documented.
- Deliverables are ready for governance review.

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Architect is responsible for ensuring that all Target State deliverables are complete, accurate, and aligned with the approved Strategy and Current State outputs.

Only completed deliverables should be submitted for governance review through **TS-DG-01**.

Following approval, these deliverables become the approved architectural baseline used during the Architecture stage.

# Summary

The Target State deliverables define the organisation's future-state enterprise architecture and provide the architectural vision required to guide transformation.

Following successful governance approval through **TS-DG-01**, these deliverables become the approved Target State outputs and provide the foundation for the Architecture stage.
