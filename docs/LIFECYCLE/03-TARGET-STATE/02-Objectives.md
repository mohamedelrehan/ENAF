# Objectives

The objective of the Target State stage is to define a future enterprise architecture that aligns business strategy with the capabilities required to achieve long-term organizational goals.

Building upon the Strategy and Current State stages, this phase establishes a shared vision of the future enterprise by defining how the organization should operate, which capabilities it requires, and how business and technology should evolve together.

The Target State provides the architectural direction that guides all subsequent design, planning, and implementation activities.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Target State stage has the following primary objectives:

| Objective | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Define the Future Vision | Establish a clear and shared view of the future enterprise aligned with the approved business strategy. |
| Design Future Capabilities | Identify the business capabilities required to support future products, services, and operating models. |
| Define the Target Operating Model | Describe how the organization will operate across people, processes, technology, governance, and service delivery. |
| Design the Future Enterprise Architecture | Define the desired future architecture across all Enterprise Design Domains. |
| Address Current State Gaps | Ensure the future design resolves the risks, limitations, technical debt, and capability gaps identified during the Current State assessment. |
| Align Business and Technology | Ensure business priorities, enterprise architecture, and technology investments support the same strategic outcomes. |
| Establish Architectural Direction | Define principles, standards, and architectural patterns that guide future solution design and delivery. |
| Enable Transformation Planning | Provide the architectural foundation for developing the transformation roadmap and implementation plans. |

---

# Business Objectives

From a business perspective, the Target State aims to:

- Define the future business operating model.
- Improve organizational agility.
- Enable business growth and innovation.
- Enhance customer and employee experiences.
- Increase operational efficiency.
- Support regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Improve enterprise resilience.
- Enable sustainable long-term transformation.

---

# Enterprise Architecture Objectives

From an architectural perspective, the Target State seeks to:

- Design an integrated enterprise architecture.
- Improve alignment between business capabilities and technology services.
- Simplify the enterprise landscape.
- Reduce architectural complexity.
- Promote reuse and standardization.
- Define future architectural principles.
- Improve interoperability across enterprise platforms.
- Support scalability and future business change.

---

# Technology Objectives

The future technology landscape should:

- Modernize legacy environments where appropriate.
- Increase cloud adoption where it delivers business value.
- Improve application integration.
- Strengthen cybersecurity and Zero Trust capabilities.
- Increase automation across infrastructure and operations.
- Improve observability and operational intelligence.
- Enhance service availability and resilience.
- Enable secure digital collaboration.

Technology decisions should always support business outcomes rather than become objectives in their own right.

---

# Transformation Objectives

The Target State also establishes objectives that enable successful transformation.

These include:

- Prioritizing business value over technology replacement.
- Defining achievable future-state architectures.
- Supporting phased transformation rather than large-scale replacement.
- Minimizing business disruption during change.
- Identifying opportunities for capability consolidation.
- Reducing technical debt over time.
- Creating measurable transformation outcomes.
- Providing a clear architectural destination for all delivery teams.

---

# Success Criteria

The Target State is considered successful when it:

| Success Measure | Expected Outcome |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Strategic Alignment | The future architecture directly supports approved business objectives. |
| Shared Vision | Business and technology stakeholders agree on the future enterprise direction. |
| Complete Enterprise Coverage | All Enterprise Design Domains are represented within the Target State. |
| Gap Resolution | The future design addresses the key findings identified during the Current State assessment. |
| Architectural Consistency | Common principles and standards are defined across the enterprise. |
| Business Value | The Target State clearly demonstrates measurable business outcomes. |
| Executive Approval | The future architecture is formally approved by business and technology leadership. |
| Roadmap Readiness | The Target State provides sufficient direction to begin Architecture and Roadmap development. |

---

# Relationship to the ENAF Lifecycle

The objectives of the Target State stage create the bridge between enterprise assessment and enterprise design.

The stage consumes:

- Strategic objectives from the Strategy stage.
- Baseline findings from the Current State stage.

The outputs produced become the primary inputs for:

- Architecture
- Roadmap
- Implementation

This ensures every architectural decision remains traceable from business strategy through to implementation and operational excellence.

---

# Summary

The Target State stage establishes the objectives that define the organization's future enterprise.

Rather than focusing on individual technologies, these objectives align business strategy, enterprise capabilities, architecture, and governance into a single, integrated vision.

By achieving these objectives, the organization creates a clear architectural destination that guides solution design, transformation planning, and successful enterprise modernization throughout the remainder of the ENAF lifecycle.
