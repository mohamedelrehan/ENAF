# Activities

The Target State stage consists of a structured set of activities that define the future-state enterprise architecture required to achieve the business objectives established during the Strategy stage.

These activities enable Enterprise Architects to design the desired business, application, data, technology, security, and network architectures while addressing the gaps and opportunities identified during the Current State Assessment.

Each activity contributes to one or more Target State deliverables that collectively describe the future operating model, architectural capabilities, technology direction, and transformation vision.

Although organisations may adapt these activities to suit their governance model, the overall sequence should remain consistent to ensure a structured, repeatable, and traceable approach to enterprise transformation.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Target State activities is to design a future-state enterprise architecture that aligns with business strategy, addresses the findings of the Current State Assessment, and provides a clear architectural vision for the organisation.

By completing these activities, Enterprise Architects produce the Target State deliverables required for governance review and approval before progressing to the Architecture stage.

---

# Activity 1 – Define the Future Business Architecture

## Objective

Define the future business architecture required to support the organisation's strategic objectives and transformation goals.

---

### Typical Activities

- Review approved business objectives.
- Review business capabilities.
- Define future business processes.
- Define future operating model.
- Identify required organisational capabilities.
- Validate business outcomes.

---

### Questions to Ask

- How should the organisation operate in the future?
- Which business capabilities must be improved?
- Which new capabilities are required?
- How will business processes change?
- Which strategic objectives must be supported?

---

### Participants

- Executive Sponsor
- Business Leadership
- Enterprise Architect
- Business Owners

---

### Deliverables Produced

| Deliverable | Contribution |
|-------------|--------------|
| DEL-TS-01 Future Business Architecture | Defines the future business architecture. |
| DEL-TS-09 Target State Summary | Summarises the future business vision. |

# Activity 8 – Validate the Target State

## Objective

Validate that the proposed Target State satisfies business objectives, addresses the Current State findings, and provides a practical foundation for architecture design.

---

### Typical Activities

- Review all Target State deliverables.
- Validate alignment with business objectives.
- Confirm stakeholder agreement.
- Validate architectural consistency.
- Confirm feasibility.
- Prepare the Target State deliverables for governance review.

---

### Validation Questions

- Does the Target State support the business objectives?
- Does it address the identified gaps?
- Is the future architecture achievable?
- Is stakeholder agreement confirmed?
- Are all Target State deliverables complete?

If the answer to any of these questions is **No**, additional Target State work should be completed before requesting governance approval.

# Summary

The Target State stage is complete when all Target State activities have been performed and the required Target State deliverables have been produced.

These deliverables provide the business and architectural evidence required for the Target State Decision Gate (TS-DG-01), where they are formally reviewed before progressing to the Architecture stage.
