# Purpose

The Implementation stage transforms the approved Enterprise Roadmap into coordinated business change through structured programmes, projects, and delivery teams.

Following approval of the Roadmap through **RM-DG-01**, the organisation has agreed:

- Why transformation is required.
- The current operating environment.
- The Target State.
- The Enterprise Architecture.
- The transformation roadmap.
- The implementation priorities.

The remaining challenge is no longer planning.

The challenge is delivering transformation successfully.

The Implementation stage provides the governance and execution model that enables the organisation to deliver complex transformation programmes while maintaining alignment with business strategy, enterprise architecture, and operational objectives.

Rather than focusing on individual technical deployments, this stage focuses on managing enterprise transformation through programmes, projects, Agile delivery teams, governance, quality assurance, business readiness, and controlled releases.

It answers questions such as:

- How should transformation initiatives be organised?
- How are programmes governed?
- How do projects align with the Enterprise Architecture?
- How are Agile teams coordinated?
- How is business value measured?
- How are risks managed during delivery?
- How is transformation controlled across multiple workstreams?

The Implementation stage ensures that every transformation initiative progresses from an approved roadmap into delivered business capabilities.

---

# Objectives

The Implementation stage aims to:

- Convert approved initiatives into transformation programmes.
- Organise programmes into manageable projects.
- Coordinate delivery across multiple workstreams.
- Ensure every project aligns with the approved Enterprise Architecture.
- Support Agile and traditional delivery approaches.
- Manage programme dependencies and risks.
- Deliver business capabilities incrementally.
- Maintain governance throughout implementation.
- Prepare the organisation for operational transition.
- Realise measurable business value.

---

# Scope

The Implementation stage governs the execution of enterprise transformation.

Typical scope includes:

- Programme management.
- Project governance.
- Agile delivery.
- Release planning.
- Architecture assurance.
- Solution governance.
- Business change management.
- Resource coordination.
- Vendor coordination.
- Delivery risk management.
- Quality assurance.
- Business readiness.
- Operational transition.

Technical implementation activities remain the responsibility of the individual engineering and delivery teams.

# Business Value

A structured implementation model enables the organisation to:

- Deliver transformation in a controlled manner.
- Maintain alignment between projects and enterprise architecture.
- Coordinate multiple programmes across the organisation.
- Reduce delivery risk.
- Improve governance and executive visibility.
- Deliver business value incrementally.
- Improve collaboration across business and technology teams.
- Accelerate transformation while maintaining quality.

# Enterprise Transformation Delivery Model

Within ENAF, implementation is delivered through a structured hierarchy.

```text
Enterprise Transformation
            │
            ▼
Transformation Programmes
            │
            ▼
Projects
            │
            ▼
Delivery Teams
            │
            ▼
Agile Iterations / Releases
            │
            ▼
Business Capabilities
            │
            ▼
Business Value
```

Each level has a distinct responsibility.

- **Transformation** delivers the enterprise vision.
- **Programmes** coordinate related business initiatives.
- **Projects** deliver specific capabilities.
- **Delivery Teams** build and deploy solutions.
- **Agile iterations and releases** provide incremental value.
- **Business capabilities** improve organisational performance.

# Summary

The Implementation stage is the enterprise transformation delivery engine of ENAF.

Rather than describing how individual technologies are deployed, it explains how organisations successfully execute transformation through governed programmes, coordinated projects, Agile delivery teams, and controlled releases.

By maintaining alignment between business strategy, enterprise architecture, roadmap priorities, and delivery execution, the Implementation stage ensures that transformation produces measurable business outcomes while reducing delivery risk and maintaining organisational control.
