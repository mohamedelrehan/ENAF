# Implementation Decision Gate

## Purpose

The Implementation Decision Gate marks the formal completion of the Implementation stage and authorises the transition of delivered capabilities into the Operations stage.

This decision gate confirms that the approved roadmap has been successfully executed, architectural requirements have been met, governance obligations have been satisfied, and the organisation is operationally prepared to assume ownership of the delivered services.

Successful completion of this gate demonstrates that the transformation has progressed from implementation into sustainable business operations.

---

# Objective

The Implementation Decision Gate verifies that:

- Approved transformation initiatives have been delivered.
- The implemented solution aligns with the approved Target Architecture.
- Business requirements have been satisfied.
- Governance requirements have been met.
- Operational teams are prepared to support the solution.
- Risks have been assessed and managed.
- Business value can now begin to be realised through operational use.

---

# Entry Criteria

Before the Decision Gate can begin, the following should be complete:

- Implementation activities have been completed.
- Programme deliverables have been approved.
- Project deliverables have been approved.
- Architecture compliance has been confirmed.
- Testing has successfully completed.
- Deployment has been completed.
- Operational readiness assessment has been completed.
- Outstanding issues have been reviewed.

---

# Decision Criteria

The following questions should be answered positively before approval.

## Business Alignment

- Have the agreed business objectives been delivered?
- Do delivered capabilities support the approved transformation initiatives?
- Have business stakeholders accepted the implementation?

---

## Architecture Compliance

- Does the implementation conform to the approved architecture?
- Have architecture principles been maintained?
- Have approved standards been followed?
- Have all architectural exceptions been formally recorded and approved?

---

## Delivery Quality

- Have implementation milestones been completed?
- Has deployment been successfully validated?
- Have agreed acceptance criteria been met?
- Have outstanding defects been assessed and accepted where appropriate?

---

## Testing

- Has system testing completed successfully?
- Has User Acceptance Testing (UAT) been completed?
- Have security tests passed?
- Have resilience and recovery tests been completed where required?
- Have performance objectives been achieved?

---

## Operational Readiness

- Are operational teams prepared to support the solution?
- Have operational procedures been documented?
- Have monitoring and alerting been configured?
- Have backup and recovery procedures been validated?
- Has knowledge transfer been completed?

---

## Governance

- Have governance reviews been completed?
- Have required approvals been obtained?
- Have risks been accepted or mitigated?
- Have compliance requirements been satisfied?
- Have audit records been completed?

---

## Business Value

- Are expected business benefits measurable?
- Have success measures been established?
- Has benefits ownership been assigned?
- Is post-implementation review scheduled?

---

# Decision Outcomes

## Approved

The Implementation stage is formally completed.

The solution is authorised to transition into operational ownership.

The organisation proceeds to the Operations stage.

---

## Approved with Conditions

Implementation is substantially complete.

Minor issues remain but do not prevent operational use.

Conditions must be resolved within agreed timescales and tracked through governance.

---

## Deferred

Additional work is required before operational transition.

Outstanding actions are assigned and the Decision Gate is reconvened once they have been completed.

---

## Rejected

Implementation does not satisfy the required quality, governance, or operational criteria.

The solution returns to the appropriate implementation activities until deficiencies have been resolved.

---

# Participants

Typical participants include:

- Executive Sponsor
- Business Owner
- Enterprise Architect
- Domain Architects
- Programme Manager
- Project Manager
- Technical Lead
- Operations Manager
- Information Security Representative
- Risk and Compliance Representative
- Service Management Representative

The governance body may vary depending on organisational structure.

---

# Required Evidence

Typical evidence presented during the Decision Gate includes:

- Programme Status Report
- Project Closure Report
- Architecture Compliance Assessment
- Test Results
- Deployment Report
- Operational Readiness Assessment
- Risk Register
- Issue Register
- Benefits Realisation Plan
- Governance Approval Records

All evidence should demonstrate readiness for operational ownership.

---

# Exit Criteria

The Decision Gate is complete when:

- The implementation has been formally accepted.
- Governance approvals have been recorded.
- Operational ownership has been transferred.
- Support responsibilities have been assigned.
- Benefits tracking has commenced.
- The Operations stage has been authorised.

---
