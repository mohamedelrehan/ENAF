# Activities

## Purpose

The Activities within the Implementation stage define how approved transformation initiatives are executed in a structured, governed, and repeatable manner.

Once the Roadmap has been approved, implementation focuses on converting strategic initiatives into coordinated programmes, projects, delivery activities, and business capabilities.

These activities ensure that enterprise transformation remains aligned with business strategy, Enterprise Architecture, governance requirements, and operational objectives throughout the execution lifecycle.

Rather than describing technical deployment tasks, the Implementation stage defines the enterprise processes used to successfully deliver transformation.

---

# Implementation Activity Model

Enterprise transformation follows a structured delivery model.

```text
Approved Initiative
        │
        ▼
Programme Establishment
        │
        ▼
Project Delivery
        │
        ▼
Agile Execution
        │
        ▼
Release Management
        │
        ▼
Business Capability Delivered
        │
        ▼
Operational Transition
```

Each activity builds upon the previous one while maintaining governance, architectural alignment, and business value.

---

# Activity 1 — Establish Transformation Programmes

Approved initiatives are grouped into transformation programmes that deliver related business capabilities.

Activities include:

- Define programme scope.
- Assign programme sponsors.
- Appoint programme managers.
- Establish governance.
- Confirm funding.
- Define programme objectives.
- Establish success measures.

Outputs:

- Approved Transformation Programme
- Programme Governance Structure

---

# Activity 2 — Initiate Projects

Each programme is divided into manageable projects that deliver specific business capabilities.

Activities include:

- Define project scope.
- Confirm project objectives.
- Assign project managers.
- Identify stakeholders.
- Create project plans.
- Allocate resources.
- Establish project governance.

Outputs:

- Approved Projects
- Project Delivery Plans

---

# Activity 3 — Validate Architectural Alignment

Before implementation begins, each project must be validated against the approved Enterprise Architecture.

Activities include:

- Review solution architecture.
- Validate technology standards.
- Review security requirements.
- Confirm compliance.
- Review design decisions.
- Resolve architecture deviations.

Outputs:

- Architecture Compliance Approval
- Updated Design Decisions

---

# Activity 4 — Plan Delivery

Projects prepare detailed execution plans before delivery starts.

Activities include:

- Build delivery backlog.
- Prioritise work.
- Identify dependencies.
- Define milestones.
- Prepare release schedule.
- Confirm delivery resources.

Outputs:

- Delivery Backlog
- Release Plan
- Delivery Schedule

---

# Activity 5 — Execute Delivery

Delivery teams implement the approved solution using the organisation's chosen delivery methodology.

Typical approaches include:

- Agile
- Scrum
- Kanban
- SAFe
- Hybrid Delivery
- Traditional Project Delivery

Activities include:

- Sprint planning.
- Development.
- Configuration.
- Integration.
- Testing.
- Demonstrations.
- Continuous improvement.

Outputs:

- Completed Solution Components
- Incremental Business Capabilities

---

# Activity 6 — Manage Programme Governance

Implementation is continuously governed throughout delivery.

Activities include:

- Programme reviews.
- Project reporting.
- Risk management.
- Budget monitoring.
- Dependency management.
- Executive reporting.
- Decision management.

Outputs:

- Programme Status Reports
- Governance Decisions
- Updated Risk Register

---

# Activity 7 — Manage Quality Assurance

Quality is validated throughout implementation rather than only at project completion.

Activities include:

- Solution reviews.
- Testing.
- Architecture assurance.
- Security validation.
- Compliance validation.
- User acceptance.

Outputs:

- Quality Reports
- Test Results
- Solution Acceptance

---

# Activity 8 — Manage Releases

Approved solution increments are packaged into controlled releases.

Activities include:

- Release planning.
- Deployment approval.
- Change coordination.
- Release validation.
- Deployment execution.
- Rollback preparation.

Outputs:

- Approved Release
- Production Deployment

---

# Activity 9 — Prepare Operational Transition

Before implementation is considered complete, operational teams must be prepared to support the delivered capability.

Activities include:

- Operational documentation.
- Knowledge transfer.
- Support readiness.
- Service documentation.
- Training.
- Support acceptance.

Outputs:

- Operational Readiness
- Service Documentation
- Support Handover

---

# Activity 10 — Measure Business Value

Implementation concludes by confirming that expected business outcomes have been achieved.

Activities include:

- Measure KPIs.
- Validate business outcomes.
- Compare expected benefits.
- Identify lessons learned.
- Record improvement opportunities.
- Report transformation performance.

Outputs:

- Benefits Realisation Report
- Lessons Learned
- Programme Closure Report

# Success Indicators

Implementation activities are considered successful when:

- Transformation programmes are established.
- Projects remain aligned with Enterprise Architecture.
- Governance is maintained.
- Delivery risks are managed.
- Business capabilities are delivered incrementally.
- Releases are successfully deployed.
- Operational teams accept ownership.
- Expected business benefits are achieved.

---

# Summary

The activities of the Implementation stage provide the structured execution model for enterprise transformation.

By organising transformation into programmes, projects, Agile delivery, governance, quality assurance, controlled releases, and operational transition, ENAF ensures that strategic initiatives are transformed into measurable business capabilities while maintaining alignment with enterprise objectives and architectural principles.
