# Objectives

## Purpose

The objectives of the Implementation stage define the outcomes that guide successful enterprise transformation delivery.

Following approval of the Enterprise Roadmap, the organisation enters the execution phase where strategic initiatives are delivered through programmes, projects, Agile teams, and controlled releases.

These objectives ensure that transformation remains aligned with business strategy, Enterprise Architecture, governance principles, and measurable business value throughout implementation.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Implementation stage aims to achieve the following objectives.

## 1. Deliver Enterprise Transformation

Execute the approved transformation roadmap by converting strategic initiatives into coordinated programmes and projects that deliver measurable business outcomes.

Expected outcome:

- Transformation initiatives are successfully delivered according to the approved roadmap.

---

## 2. Maintain Strategic Alignment

Ensure every programme, project, and release continues to support the strategic objectives approved during the Strategy stage.

Expected outcome:

- All implementation activities demonstrate clear traceability to business strategy.

---

## 3. Maintain Architectural Alignment

Ensure all delivered solutions comply with the approved Enterprise Architecture, design principles, technology standards, and governance decisions.

Expected outcome:

- Projects deliver solutions that align with the Target Architecture.

---

## 4. Govern Transformation Delivery

Provide consistent governance across programmes, projects, suppliers, and delivery teams to ensure quality, transparency, and accountability.

Expected outcome:

- Transformation progresses through controlled governance and decision-making.

---

## 5. Coordinate Programme Delivery

Manage multiple programmes and projects as an integrated transformation portfolio rather than isolated initiatives.

Expected outcome:

- Dependencies, risks, schedules, and resources remain coordinated across the enterprise.

---

## 6. Support Agile Delivery

Enable Agile, iterative, and incremental delivery while maintaining enterprise governance and architectural consistency.

Expected outcome:

- Agile delivery teams operate within a controlled enterprise framework.

---

## 7. Deliver Incremental Business Value

Prioritise delivery based on business value rather than technical completion, ensuring benefits are realised throughout the transformation journey.

Expected outcome:

- Business capabilities are delivered incrementally through controlled releases.

---

## 8. Manage Risk and Change

Identify, assess, and manage implementation risks, organisational change, technical dependencies, and delivery challenges before they impact business operations.

Expected outcome:

- Transformation risks remain within acceptable organisational tolerance.

---

## 9. Prepare for Operational Transition

Ensure delivered capabilities are fully prepared for handover into operational support, including documentation, training, support processes, and service readiness.

Expected outcome:

- Solutions transition into Operations with minimal disruption.

---

## 10. Measure Transformation Success

Monitor programme performance using defined KPIs, benefits, milestones, quality metrics, and governance reporting.

Expected outcome:

- Executive stakeholders have clear visibility into transformation progress and business value.

---

# Success Criteria

The Implementation stage is considered successful when:

- Approved programmes are established.
- Projects remain aligned with the Enterprise Architecture.
- Delivery milestones are achieved.
- Risks are effectively managed.
- Governance is maintained throughout implementation.
- Business capabilities are delivered incrementally.
- Operational teams are prepared for service transition.
- Expected business benefits are realised.
- Executive stakeholders maintain confidence in delivery progress.

---

# Relationship to Other Lifecycle Stages

The objectives of this stage directly support:

| Previous Stage | Contribution |
|----------------|--------------|
| Strategy | Delivers strategic objectives. |
| Current State | Resolves identified gaps and limitations. |
| Target State | Implements the approved future operating model. |
| Architecture | Delivers approved architectural designs and standards. |
| Roadmap | Executes approved programmes, projects, and delivery waves. |

The outputs of this stage become the operational capabilities managed during the Operations stage.

---

# Summary

The objectives of the Implementation stage provide the foundation for successful enterprise transformation delivery.

Rather than measuring success solely by project completion, ENAF measures implementation success by strategic alignment, architectural compliance, governed programme execution, incremental business value, operational readiness, and measurable business outcomes.
