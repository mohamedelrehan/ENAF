# Decision Gate

The Roadmap Decision Gate (**RM-DG-01**) is the formal governance checkpoint that concludes the Roadmap stage of the ENAF lifecycle.

Its purpose is to verify that the enterprise transformation roadmap has been completed, reviewed, prioritised, and approved before progressing to the Implementation stage.

The Decision Gate confirms that the roadmap provides a realistic, governed, and executable plan for delivering the approved Enterprise Architecture while balancing business priorities, investment, resources, organisational readiness, and delivery risk.

No implementation activities should begin until the Roadmap Decision Gate has been successfully approved.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of **RM-DG-01** is to:

- Verify that all Roadmap activities have been completed.
- Confirm that all required Roadmap deliverables have been produced.
- Validate alignment with the approved Strategy, Current State, Target State, and Enterprise Architecture.
- Confirm that transformation initiatives have been prioritised.
- Ensure implementation waves, milestones, and dependencies have been defined.
- Authorise progression to the Implementation stage.

# Review Criteria

The Decision Gate should confirm that:

- The roadmap supports the approved Strategy.
- The roadmap delivers the approved Target State.
- The roadmap follows the approved Enterprise Architecture.
- Transformation initiatives have been appropriately prioritised.
- Implementation waves are logical and achievable.
- Dependencies have been identified and documented.
- Resource and investment assumptions are realistic.
- Risks have been assessed and mitigation strategies defined.
- Governance and reporting mechanisms are established.
- Stakeholders agree with the proposed transformation plan.
- The organisation is ready to begin implementation.

---

# Decision Outcomes

The governance board should record one of the following outcomes.

| Decision | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Approved** | The transformation roadmap is approved and the Implementation stage may begin. |
| **Approved with Conditions** | Minor actions remain. Implementation may begin once the agreed conditions have been completed. |
| **Deferred** | Additional information or clarification is required before a decision can be made. |
| **Rejected** | Significant issues have been identified. The Roadmap stage must be revisited before resubmission. |

---

# Participants

The Decision Gate should include representatives appropriate to the organisation, typically including:

- Executive Sponsor
- Business Leadership
- Enterprise Architect
- Transformation Office
- Programme Management Office (PMO)
- Portfolio Management Representatives
- Finance Representatives
- Technology Leadership
- Governance or Transformation Review Board

---

# Approved Outputs

Following successful approval through **RM-DG-01**, the Roadmap deliverables become the official approved outputs of the Roadmap stage.

| Output ID | Approved Output |
|-----------|-----------------|
| RM-OUT-01 | Approved Transformation Initiative Catalogue |
| RM-OUT-02 | Approved Prioritised Transformation Portfolio |
| RM-OUT-03 | Approved Dependency Model |
| RM-OUT-04 | Approved Implementation Wave Plan |
| RM-OUT-05 | Approved Enterprise Transformation Roadmap |
| RM-OUT-06 | Approved Resource and Investment Plan |
| RM-OUT-07 | Approved Roadmap Risk Register |
| RM-OUT-08 | Approved Governance and Milestone Plan |
| RM-OUT-09 | Approved Roadmap Summary |

These approved outputs become the mandatory inputs for the Implementation stage.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Enterprise Architect, Transformation Office, and Programme Management Office (PMO) are responsible for:

- Presenting the completed transformation roadmap.
- Demonstrating alignment with the approved Enterprise Architecture.
- Explaining initiative prioritisation and implementation sequencing.
- Presenting resource, investment, and dependency assumptions.
- Documenting transformation risks and mitigation strategies.
- Recording governance decisions and agreed actions resulting from the Decision Gate.

The governance board is responsible for approving, deferring, or rejecting the proposed enterprise transformation roadmap.

# Summary

The Roadmap Decision Gate (**RM-DG-01**) provides formal governance approval for the organisation's enterprise transformation roadmap.

Following successful approval, the Roadmap deliverables become approved Roadmap outputs and establish the authorised implementation plan that guides programmes and projects through the Implementation stage.
