# Deliverables

The Roadmap stage produces the approved transformation planning deliverables that define how the organisation will implement its Enterprise Architecture through a structured sequence of programmes, projects, implementation waves, and milestones.

These deliverables translate architectural direction into an executable transformation plan that balances business priorities, dependencies, investment, organisational readiness, and delivery risk.

Together, these deliverables provide the organisation with a single, governed roadmap that guides implementation from the current state to the approved Target State.

All Roadmap deliverables are reviewed during the Roadmap Decision Gate (**RM-DG-01**) before the Implementation stage begins.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Roadmap deliverables is to:

- Translate Enterprise Architecture into implementation planning.
- Prioritise transformation initiatives.
- Define implementation waves.
- Establish delivery milestones.
- Identify dependencies.
- Support investment planning.
- Guide enterprise-wide transformation.
- Provide governance for implementation.

---

# Deliverables

## DEL-RM-01 – Transformation Initiative Catalogue

### Purpose

Defines every initiative required to achieve the approved Target State.

### Typical Contents

- Initiative Register
- Initiative Description
- Strategic Alignment
- Business Value
- Initiative Owner
- High-Level Scope
- Priority Classification

---

## DEL-RM-02 – Prioritised Transformation Portfolio

### Purpose

Provides the prioritised list of programmes and projects that make up the enterprise transformation portfolio.

### Typical Contents

- Programme Portfolio
- Project Portfolio
- Initiative Priorities
- Business Value Assessment
- Executive Priorities
- Investment Ranking

---

## DEL-RM-03 – Dependency Model

### Purpose

Documents the dependencies between initiatives, programmes, projects, business capabilities, and technology platforms.

### Typical Contents

- Business Dependencies
- Technology Dependencies
- Data Dependencies
- Security Dependencies
- Infrastructure Dependencies
- External Dependencies
- Critical Path Analysis

---

## DEL-RM-04 – Implementation Wave Plan

### Purpose

Organises transformation initiatives into logical implementation waves.

### Typical Contents

- Wave Definitions
- Programme Allocation
- Project Sequencing
- Wave Objectives
- Business Outcomes
- Delivery Schedule
- High-Level Timeline

---

## DEL-RM-05 – Enterprise Transformation Roadmap

### Purpose

Provides the overall roadmap for delivering the enterprise transformation.

### Typical Contents

- Executive Roadmap
- Transformation Timeline
- Programme Overview
- Major Milestones
- Implementation Waves
- Strategic Outcomes
- Delivery Schedule

---

## DEL-RM-06 – Resource and Investment Plan

### Purpose

Defines the resources, funding, and organisational capacity required to execute the roadmap.

### Typical Contents

- Resource Requirements
- Skills Assessment
- Budget Overview
- Investment Planning
- Capacity Planning
- Vendor Requirements
- Funding Assumptions

---

## DEL-RM-07 – Roadmap Risk Register

### Purpose

Identifies the strategic and delivery risks that could affect successful implementation.

### Typical Contents

- Strategic Risks
- Programme Risks
- Delivery Risks
- Technology Risks
- Security Risks
- Financial Risks
- Risk Mitigation Actions

---

## DEL-RM-08 – Governance and Milestone Plan

### Purpose

Defines the governance checkpoints and major milestones that will monitor transformation progress.

### Typical Contents

- Governance Structure
- Decision Gates
- Executive Reviews
- Stage Milestones
- Reporting Schedule
- Escalation Process
- Success Criteria

---

## DEL-RM-09 – Roadmap Summary

### Purpose

Provides a consolidated summary of the Roadmap stage.

This document brings together the key outcomes from all Roadmap deliverables and prepares the stage for governance review.

### Typical Contents

- Executive Summary
- Transformation Overview
- Priority Initiatives
- Implementation Waves
- Major Dependencies
- Investment Summary
- Key Risks
- Expected Business Outcomes

# Deliverable Validation

Before requesting governance approval, verify that:

- All Roadmap activities have been completed.
- Every required DEL-RM deliverable has been produced.
- Transformation initiatives have been prioritised.
- Dependencies have been documented.
- Implementation waves have been defined.
- Resources and investment have been validated.
- Risks have been assessed and documented.
- Governance milestones have been established.
- Stakeholders have reviewed the roadmap.
- Deliverables are ready for governance review.

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Architect, Transformation Office, and Programme Management Office (PMO) are jointly responsible for ensuring that all Roadmap deliverables are complete, governed, and aligned with the approved Strategy, Current State, Target State, and Enterprise Architecture.

Only completed deliverables should be submitted for governance review through **RM-DG-01**.

Following approval, these deliverables become the approved transformation roadmap used during the Implementation stage.

# Summary

The Roadmap deliverables transform the approved Enterprise Architecture into a governed and executable transformation plan.

By defining initiatives, priorities, dependencies, implementation waves, investment requirements, governance, and business milestones, these deliverables provide a clear path from architectural intent to successful execution.

Following successful approval through **RM-DG-01**, these deliverables become the approved Roadmap outputs and provide the foundation for the Implementation stage.
