# Activities

The Roadmap stage converts the approved Enterprise Architecture into a structured, prioritised, and executable transformation plan.

Unlike the previous stages, which define strategy and architecture, the Roadmap stage focuses on **planning the journey**. It determines what will be delivered, in what order, over what timeframe, and through which programmes and projects.

The activities performed during this stage ensure that transformation is realistic, governed, aligned with business priorities, and achievable within the organisation's capabilities.

---

# Activity 1 – Identify Transformation Initiatives

## Objective

Identify all initiatives required to move the organisation from the approved Current State to the approved Target State.

## Typical Activities

- Review approved Architecture outputs.
- Identify required business initiatives.
- Identify technology initiatives.
- Identify security improvements.
- Identify operational improvements.
- Group related initiatives into programmes.
- Remove duplicate initiatives.

## Questions

- What changes are required?
- Which initiatives create the greatest business value?
- Which initiatives are mandatory?
- Which initiatives are optional?
- Are all architecture domains represented?

## Participants

- Enterprise Architect
- Business Leadership
- Programme Management Office (PMO)
- Domain Architects
- Transformation Office

## Deliverables Produced

- Initial Transformation Initiative Catalogue

---

# Activity 2 – Prioritise Initiatives

## Objective

Prioritise transformation initiatives according to business value, strategic importance, risk, dependencies, and organisational readiness.

## Typical Activities

- Evaluate business value.
- Assess strategic alignment.
- Review regulatory requirements.
- Consider customer impact.
- Evaluate technical complexity.
- Assess implementation risk.
- Prioritise initiatives.

## Questions

- Which initiatives deliver the greatest value?
- Which initiatives reduce the highest risks?
- Which initiatives are business critical?
- Which initiatives can be deferred?

## Participants

- Executive Sponsor
- Enterprise Architect
- Business Leadership
- PMO
- Finance Representatives

## Deliverables Produced

- Prioritised Transformation Initiative List

---

# Activity 3 – Analyse Dependencies

## Objective

Identify dependencies that influence the sequencing and timing of transformation initiatives.

## Typical Activities

- Identify business dependencies.
- Identify application dependencies.
- Identify data dependencies.
- Identify infrastructure dependencies.
- Identify security dependencies.
- Review vendor constraints.
- Document implementation prerequisites.

## Questions

- Which initiatives depend on others?
- Which capabilities must be delivered first?
- What external dependencies exist?
- What activities can run in parallel?

## Participants

- Enterprise Architect
- Domain Architects
- Programme Managers
- Technical Leads

## Deliverables Produced

- Dependency Model
- Sequencing Analysis

---

# Activity 4 – Define Implementation Waves

## Objective

Organise initiatives into logical implementation waves that balance business value, delivery capacity, and risk.

## Typical Activities

- Group initiatives into phases.
- Define implementation waves.
- Balance programme workloads.
- Align business readiness.
- Validate delivery capacity.
- Plan incremental value delivery.

## Questions

- What should be delivered first?
- How many implementation waves are required?
- Can business disruption be minimised?
- Does each wave provide measurable value?

## Participants

- Enterprise Architect
- PMO
- Programme Managers
- Business Leadership
- Delivery Teams

## Deliverables Produced

- Implementation Wave Plan

---

# Activity 5 – Develop the Enterprise Roadmap

## Objective

Develop a high-level roadmap that defines the overall transformation journey.

## Typical Activities

- Build the transformation timeline.
- Position implementation waves.
- Define milestones.
- Align programmes and projects.
- Identify governance checkpoints.
- Validate roadmap assumptions.

## Questions

- Does the roadmap achieve the Target State?
- Is the timeline realistic?
- Are business priorities reflected?
- Are governance checkpoints included?

## Participants

- Enterprise Architect
- PMO
- Executive Sponsor
- Business Leadership

## Deliverables Produced

- Enterprise Transformation Roadmap

---

# Activity 6 – Validate Resources, Investment, and Risks

## Objective

Confirm that the roadmap is achievable using the organisation's available resources, funding, and delivery capability.

## Typical Activities

- Review delivery capacity.
- Assess resource availability.
- Validate investment assumptions.
- Identify programme risks.
- Review organisational readiness.
- Update roadmap where necessary.

## Questions

- Is the roadmap achievable?
- Are sufficient resources available?
- Are investment assumptions realistic?
- Have significant risks been addressed?

## Participants

- Executive Sponsor
- Finance
- PMO
- Enterprise Architect
- Business Leadership

## Deliverables Produced

- Resource Assessment
- Investment Assessment
- Roadmap Risk Register

---

# Activity 7 – Validate the Roadmap

## Objective

Confirm that the roadmap is complete, aligned with enterprise objectives, and ready for governance approval.

## Typical Activities

- Review roadmap completeness.
- Validate implementation waves.
- Review dependencies.
- Confirm milestone alignment.
- Review stakeholder feedback.
- Prepare governance submission.

## Questions

- Does the roadmap support the approved Architecture?
- Are priorities agreed?
- Have stakeholders approved the roadmap?
- Is the roadmap ready for governance review?

## Participants

- Enterprise Architect
- Executive Sponsor
- PMO
- Governance Board
- Business Leadership

## Deliverables Produced

- Final Roadmap Submission Package

# Activity Validation

Before progressing to the Decision Gate, confirm that:

- All transformation initiatives have been identified.
- Initiatives have been prioritised.
- Dependencies have been documented.
- Implementation waves have been defined.
- The enterprise roadmap has been completed.
- Major milestones have been established.
- Resource and investment planning has been validated.
- Programme risks have been documented.
- Stakeholders have reviewed the roadmap.
- The roadmap is ready for governance approval through **RM-DG-01**.

---

# Summary

The Roadmap stage transforms enterprise architecture into an executable transformation plan by identifying initiatives, prioritising work, analysing dependencies, organising implementation waves, and defining the overall transformation journey.

The completed roadmap provides executive leadership with a clear, governed plan for delivering business and technology change while balancing value, risk, resources, and organisational readiness.
