# Inputs

The Roadmap stage begins once the Enterprise Architecture has been formally approved through **AR-DG-01**.

At this point, the organisation has established:

- The business strategy that defines **why** transformation is required.
- The Current State that explains **where the organisation is today**.
- The Target State that defines **where the organisation wants to be**.
- The Enterprise Architecture that defines **how the organisation should be structured** to achieve the Target State.

The Roadmap stage now determines **when**, **in what sequence**, and **through which initiatives** the transformation will be delivered.

The quality of the Roadmap depends on the completeness and governance of the approved inputs received from the previous lifecycle stages.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Roadmap inputs is to ensure that transformation planning is based on approved business and architectural decisions rather than assumptions.

These inputs enable the organisation to:

- Prioritise transformation initiatives.
- Identify implementation dependencies.
- Define delivery waves.
- Align investment and resources.
- Manage enterprise risk.
- Produce an achievable transformation roadmap.

---

# Input Validation

Before roadmap development begins, the following should be confirmed:

- Strategy has been approved through **STR-DG-01**.
- Current State has been approved through **CS-DG-01**.
- Target State has been approved through **TS-DG-01**.
- Architecture has been approved through **AR-DG-01**.
- All approved outputs are complete and available.
- Major assumptions and constraints have been documented.
- Stakeholders agree that the organisation is ready for transformation planning.

If any prerequisite is incomplete, roadmap planning should be postponed until the relevant governance actions have been completed.

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Architect, Transformation Office, and Business Leadership are jointly responsible for validating the roadmap inputs.

Their responsibilities include:

- Confirming the use of approved lifecycle outputs.
- Verifying alignment with strategic priorities.
- Reviewing architectural dependencies.
- Validating business priorities.
- Confirming organisational readiness.
- Ensuring transformation planning follows enterprise governance.

Only validated inputs should be used to produce the enterprise transformation roadmap.

# Summary

The Roadmap stage integrates the approved outputs from Strategy, Current State, Target State, and Architecture into a single planning foundation.

These validated inputs ensure that transformation initiatives are prioritised according to business objectives, architectural guidance, implementation dependencies, and organisational readiness, enabling the development of a realistic and executable enterprise transformation roadmap.
