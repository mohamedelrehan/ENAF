# Purpose

The Roadmap stage transforms the approved Enterprise Architecture into a practical, prioritised, and executable transformation plan.

Following approval of the Architecture stage through **AR-DG-01**, the organisation has a clear understanding of:

- Why transformation is required.
- The current operating environment.
- The desired Target State.
- The enterprise architecture needed to achieve that Target State.

The remaining challenge is determining **when**, **in what order**, and **through which initiatives** the transformation should be delivered.

The Roadmap stage answers these questions by converting architectural direction into a phased implementation plan that balances business priorities, risk, dependencies, available resources, and organisational change.

Rather than defining technical designs, the Roadmap focuses on sequencing transformation activities to maximise business value while minimising delivery risk.

---

# Objectives

The Roadmap stage aims to:

- Translate the approved enterprise architecture into a realistic transformation roadmap.
- Prioritise initiatives according to business value and strategic importance.
- Identify dependencies between programmes, projects, and capabilities.
- Define implementation waves and delivery milestones.
- Align investment with organisational priorities.
- Support effective resource and budget planning.
- Reduce delivery and transformation risk.
- Provide executive visibility of the transformation journey.

---

# Scope

The Roadmap stage covers enterprise-level transformation planning, including:

- Transformation initiatives.
- Strategic programmes.
- Major projects.
- Capability delivery sequencing.
- Implementation waves.
- Dependencies.
- Business milestones.
- High-level timelines.
- Investment priorities.
- Enterprise transformation governance.

Detailed solution design and project execution are addressed during later lifecycle stages.

---

# Business Value

A structured roadmap enables the organisation to:

- Deliver transformation in manageable phases.
- Focus investment on the highest-value initiatives.
- Reduce programme risk through dependency management.
- Coordinate business and technology change.
- Improve executive decision-making.
- Provide a clear view of transformation progress.
- Support funding and portfolio management.
- Enable predictable and measurable delivery.

---

# Lifecycle Position

Within ENAF, the Roadmap stage follows the Architecture stage and precedes Implementation.

```text
Strategy
    │
    ▼
Current State
    │
    ▼
Target State
    │
    ▼
Architecture
    │
    ▼
Roadmap
    │
    ▼
Implementation
```

The Roadmap converts the approved enterprise architecture into an actionable transformation plan.

---

# Summary

The Roadmap stage bridges the gap between architecture and delivery.

It transforms strategic intent and architectural direction into a prioritised, phased transformation plan that guides implementation across the enterprise. By defining initiatives, dependencies, implementation waves, and business milestones, the Roadmap provides a clear path from architectural vision to successful execution.
